Good questions

Still wondering? Call or text (254) 418‑4542.

When are you open?

Our Manor truck is open every day, 11am to 9pm. If we sell out or close early, we post it in Today's Boil and on Facebook first.

Is crawfish in season?

Crawfish season usually runs January to June. In season, get it by the pound ($9.99/lb) or as the 5 lb Combo. Off season, try a krab & shrimp boil.

Do you deliver?

Yes! Order delivery on DoorDash. Prefer pickup? Call ahead and it'll be hot when you get here.

What butters can I choose?

Cajun, Garlic, Lemon Pepper, Fire, or our House butter. Want extra? Add more butter for $1.50.

Can I order for a party?

Absolutely. The Family Platter feeds a crew, and we take custom party orders. Text us your date and headcount.

Can I eat there?

Yes, the Manor spot has dine‑in and outdoor seating. Grab a table and dig in.
